Shiny Shell

You are a crab in a glowing deep-sea lair. Grab shiny treasure and it sticks to your shell. Fill it before the other crabs do.

  • Round lasts 3 minutes. Highest treasure score wins.
  • Gems, pearls, coins, starfish and rare golden hooks are worth more.
  • VR: left stick scuttles, right stick turns, claws grab. Squeeze to snap.
