Shiny Shell

You are a crab in a glowing deep-sea world. Explore at your own pace, grab shiny treasure, and watch it stick to your shell.

  • Free play: no timer or rounds. Treasure earns points, and the rarest finds send a beam of light into the water.
  • Plant a claw on the seabed or a rock and push to swim. Paddle with closed claws to glide.
  • VR: your arms are your legs. Plant a claw and push. Right stick turns, A kicks up. Click the right stick for music, the left stick to toggle stick walking.
